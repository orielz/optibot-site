OptiBot doesn't just suggest changes. It runs a full experiment and proves the improvement before opening a PR.

1

Daily scans analyze your CI workflows, build configurations, dependencies, and tooling to find optimization opportunities across every repo.

2

A candidate branch is created with the optimized configuration. No checkout needed — changes are applied via the GitHub API.

3

The change is tested through controlled experiments. Results are compared against real baselines to verify the improvement holds up.

4
